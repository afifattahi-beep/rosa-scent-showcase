Our Story

“Rosa was born from a single rose — its quiet strength, its tender bloom, its scent that lingers long after it is gone.”

Each blend is composed in small batches, poured by hand and sealed in a petite 6 ml bottle — a private ritual of beauty you can carry anywhere. No alcohol, no haste; only pure essence, made with devotion.
